作物遇到逆境的時候需要保護劑。難的不是做出保護劑，是在對的時間、給對的量。 現在要做到這件事，不是蓋一座很貴的廠，就是要有人一直盯著，小農兩個都做不到。

ReLeaf 想做的是一台自己會判斷的小工廠。我們改造枯草桿菌（Bacillus subtilis）， 把環境感測直接接到菌的生產開關上：土壤和天氣說作物快撐不住了，菌才開始做保護劑，做好之後分泌出來， 透過一層膜送到根那一側；不需要的時候就不做。產出跟著需求走，所以它不是把保護劑運到田裡，是把工廠放在田裡。

要證明這件事行得通，得先有一套自己信得過的植物逆境模型。

A crop under stress needs a protectant. The hard part is not making one, it is getting the right amount out at the right moment. Doing that today takes either expensive infrastructure or somebody watching all the time, and a smallholder has neither.

ReLeaf is a small factory that decides for itself. We engineer Bacillus subtilis and wire environmental sensing straight to the cell’s production switch: when the soil and the forecast say the crop is about to struggle, the bacteria make the protectant and secrete it, and it crosses a membrane to the root side. When nothing is needed, nothing is made. Output tracks need, so this does not ship a protectant to the field, it puts the factory in the field.

To show that works we first need a plant stress model we trust.

    Why BoPep4 is the one we picked. Wang et al. this year (6) validated it in both broccoli and Arabidopsis, and applying synthetic BoPep4 from outside markedly improved salt tolerance; they put it forward as a bio-stimulant for crop resilience in saline soils. What that means for us: a result like this usually takes years to reach a grower, and what holds it up is manufacturing, formulation and distribution. If the bacteria can make it at the edge of the field, a grower does not have to wait for it to be commercialised before using something the literature has just verified.

    Trehalose is the one design element the agar work deleted outright. The first pre-test already showed it: ½ MS control averaged 51.7 mm, and 1 mM trehalose brought that down to 14.6 mm. We dropped to 0.5 mM and set 2 still showed inhibition, then ran 0.125 to 0.5 mM as set 3. By sets 5 and 6, testing 1, 5 and 10 mM against salt, whatever it did under stress showed up in chlorophyll and not in root length (Figure 2). It never made it into the circuit.

    4.2　鹽的劑量反應4.2　The salt dose response

    0 到 150 mM 之間根長掉了 43 倍，單獨加鹽那四組的 SD 都在 4.5 mm 以內。 75 mM 那一格是我們現在用來做逆境的濃度，它把根長壓到對照的 46 %，植株還活著、還量得動。

    A 43-fold suppression from 0 to 150 mM, and the four NaCl-alone groups all sit under 4.5 mm SD. The 75 mM column is what we now use for stress: it takes root length to 46 % of control and the plants are still alive and still measurable.

    4.3　根長沒反應，葉綠素有4.3　No response in root length, a response in chlorophyll

    We were given a guess to test: under salt, trehalose is probably not acting by restoring root growth, and the published salt-tolerance work reads out on shoots, so we should be measuring shoot fresh weight and rosette area. Set 5 trial 2 is consistent with that guess. We do not have rosette area yet.

    現在跑的是 0.1× MS 加打氧。我們自己想得到的三個原因分不出來， 想跟您一起判斷到底是哪一個造成我們的植株和您們的差這麼多：

    1. 培養液配方The medium 8/25 我們同時放了 ½ MS 和 0.1× MS，0.1× 那盒當天就出現葉子白化，½ MS 那盒沒有問題。 但現在跑的是 0.1×。濃度是不是本來就該拉回去？ On 25 August we ran ½ MS and 0.1× MS side by side. The 0.1× box showed leaf whitening the same day and the ½ MS box had no problems. What we run now is the 0.1×. Should the strength go back up?
    2. eppendorf 蓋子上那層 agar 的硬度How hard the agar in the eppendorf lid is 苗是長在蓋子上那層 agar 裡的，根要穿出去才碰得到培養液。如果 agar 調太硬，根出不去， 植株就會停在那裡。這個我們沒有量過硬度，也沒有比較過不同濃度的 agar。 The seedling grows in that layer and its root has to get through it to reach the medium. If the agar is set too firm the root does not get out and the plant stalls there. We have never measured the firmness or compared different agar concentrations.
    3. 光照強度Light 我們的光週期和光強度從第一版設計就沒有填，到現在還是沒有量過。 這是三個裡面我們最沒有底的。 Our photoperiod and light intensity were left blank in the first design and we have still never measured them. Of the three this is the one we know least about.

    Q2

    打氧會不會反而讓細菌大量繁殖？如果會，怎麼避開？

    Does the aeration make the bacteria bloom? If so, how do we avoid it?

    我們現在有打氧。污染從第一個原型到現在沒有停過（第 7 節）， 而且我們已經把換液拉到 2–3 天一次，還是沒解決。氧氣進去之後，是不是等於同時餵飽了水裡的菌？ 如果打氧和乾淨兩件事互相衝突，您會怎麼取捨，或是有沒有別的供氧方式？

    We aerate. The contamination has not stopped since the first prototype (section 7) and we have already pushed the medium change to every two to three days without solving it. Does putting oxygen in feed whatever is in the water? If aeration and cleanliness pull against each other, how would you trade them off, or is there another way to get oxygen in?

    Q3

    含糖的 agar 一起進到水裡，是不是污染的來源？

    Is the sucrose in that agar what is feeding the contamination?

    我們的 agar 是 ½ MS 加 1 % 蔗糖，苗連著這塊膠一起放進水裡， 而 8/8 起連續 43 次的紀錄說這塊膠是露在培養液外面的。五個原型裡唯一沒寫到污染的 P4， 正好是唯一沒有把 agar 帶進水裡的那一版。

    想得到的做法有三個：移進去之前把 agar 沖掉、蓋子上改鋪不加蔗糖的 agar、 或是回到 P4 的濾紙。三個各有代價。您會先試哪一個？

    Our agar is ½ MS with 1 % sucrose, the seedling goes in still sitting in it, and 43 consecutive entries from 8 August say the gel is outside the lid and in the medium. P4, the only one of the five prototypes with no contamination logged, is also the only one that did not carry agar into the water.

    Three things we can think of: rinse the agar off before the seedling goes in, set the lid with sucrose-free agar, or go back to P4’s filter paper. Each costs something. Which would you try first?

    Agar 和土Agar and soil

    Q4

    鹽逆境在 agar 上，我們是把鹽水加在表面。這樣做對嗎？

    For salt stress on agar we pour the salt solution onto the surface. Is that right?

    同一批盤上我們同時做三個時間點：pre treatment（鹽之前給）、 co treatment（和鹽一起給）、post treatment（鹽之後給），加上對照。 鹽水是加在 agar 表面，不是拌進 agar 裡。

    我們不確定的是：表面加的鹽會不會沒有均勻擴散到根碰得到的地方， 或是反而在表面形成一個比配方濃度高很多的區域。如果應該拌進 agar，那 pre／co／post 這個設計要整個重想， 因為拌進去之後就沒辦法「之後再加」。

    On the same batch of plates we run three timings at once: pre-treatment before the salt, co-treatment with it, and post-treatment after, plus a control. The salt goes onto the surface of the agar rather than into it.

    What we are unsure about is whether surface-applied salt spreads evenly to where the roots are, or instead sits as a much stronger layer at the top. If it should be mixed into the agar instead, the whole pre / co / post design has to be rethought, because once it is in the agar there is no adding it later.

    Q5

    土耕的鹽逆境配 ACCD，現在的 protocol 和處理時間點合適嗎？

    For the soil salt stress with ACCD, is our protocol and its timing right?

    現在的做法：第 3、4 盤，分成前處理、同時、後處理，加上 150 mM NaCl 對照和純對照。 鹽用澆灌的，protectant 另外加。8/23 開始，那是移植後第 28 天， 而那天的照片裡植株已經抽苔開花（5.2），這個時間點我們會調整。

    想請教兩件：一是鹽水澆灌和加 protectant 的先後與間隔，現在各差 3 天，這個間距合不合理； 二是重跑的話，苗齡該抓在哪裡。您 3/27 說營養生長約 14 天之後，我們想確認在土裡是從移植那天算還是從發芽算。

    What we do now: trays 3 and 4, split into pre-, co- and post-treatment with a 150 mM NaCl control and a plain control. The salt goes on as a drench and the protectant separately. We started on 23 August, day 28 after transfer, and the photographs from that day show plants already bolted (5.2), and we will move that timing.

    Two things we would like to ask. First, the order and gap between the salt drench and the protectant, which is three days either side at the moment: is that spacing sensible? Second, if we rerun it, what seedling age to aim for. You said about fourteen days of vegetative growth; we want to check whether that is counted from the transfer or from germination once they are in soil.
