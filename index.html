What we want to try is not shipping them at all, and letting whoever needs one produce it where it is used. To show that works we first need a plant stress model we trust.     Q7

    BoPep4 是花椰菜的 elicitor peptide，我們要在阿拉伯芥上測。讀值該用根長抑制，還是鹽逆境的救援？

    BoPep4 is a broccoli elicitor peptide and we will be testing it on Arabidopsis. Should the readout be root-growth inhibition, or salinity rescue?

    六個 DNA 構築 8/20 已經下單，合成胜肽同時訂了。發表這個胜肽的那篇講的是耐鹽， 但整個 Pep 家族的定量文獻讀的都是免疫反應，多半用 medium alkalinization 或 ROS burst。 我們想拿劑量反應曲線，根長抑制最便宜也給得出曲線；但如果要對上產品的說法，該做的是鹽逆境救援。 而且 BoPep4 是 Brassica 的胜肽，接到阿拉伯芥的 PEPR1 上，跨物種本身就是一個變數。

    Six DNA constructs went in on 20 August with synthetic peptides ordered alongside. The paper that published this peptide is about salt tolerance, but the quantitative Pep literature is all immunity, mostly medium alkalinisation or the ROS burst. We want a dose-response curve, and root-growth inhibition is the cheapest thing that gives one. If the claim is about the product, salinity rescue is what matters. And BoPep4 is a Brassica peptide docking onto Arabidopsis PEPR1, so the species jump is itself a variable.

    會改變什麼：What it changes: 胜肽到貨之後第一輪做哪一個 assay，以及要不要同時跑一個 Brassica 對照物種。 Which assay runs first when the peptides arrive, and whether a Brassica species runs alongside.

    Q8

    這個水耕盤，您會先在哪裡看出問題？

    Looking at this plate, where would you expect it to fail first?

    我們自己知道的有兩個：污染從第一個原型到現在沒斷過， 以及盤會歪、中間那排會沉進培養液（6.3）。 我們想聽的是第三個，也就是我們還沒看到的那一個。您 3/27 給我們看過您們做水耕的心得， 如果那些心得裡有我們這個設計還沒處理到的，明天最想聽的是這個。

    Two we already know: contamination has not stopped since the first prototype, and the plate tilts so the middle row sinks into the medium (6.3). What we want is the third one, the one we have not seen. You showed us what you had learned from your own hydroponic work on 27 March; if anything there is still unhandled in this design, that is what we most want to hear tomorrow.

    會改變什麼：What it changes: 下一版盤的幾何，以及水耕這條線要不要繼續留在專案裡。 The geometry of the next plate, and whether the hydroponic line stays in the project at all.

    C · 種子C · Seeds

    Q9

    您願意再給我們一些種子，我們想先問該拿哪些品系；另外想跟您學怎麼收種子。

    You offered us more seed. We would like to ask which lines to take, and to learn how you collect seed.

    我們現在做的都是野生型的鹽逆境和熱逆境。 您實驗室用 translatome 找出來的那幾個抗逆境基因，突變株對鹽逆境的耐受度比較低， 如果那些品系能拿到一兩個當對照，我們的鹽逆境曲線會多一個可以定位的參考點， 不用只跟自己的對照組比。想請教的是這樣做在我們這個時間尺度上合不合理，還是先把野生型做穩比較實在。

    收種子這一邊，我們目前只做到把花序綁上支架（圖 6）。 之後怎麼套袋、什麼時候剪、怎麼乾燥、怎麼分株系不混到，都還沒做過。 這一段我們想在現場跟您學，而不是自己去查。

    Everything we run at the moment is wild type, under salt or heat. Your lab used translatome analysis to pull out stress genes whose mutants are less salt-tolerant. If we could take one or two of those lines as a reference, our salinity curve would have an anchor other than our own control. What we would like to ask is whether that is sensible on our timescale, or whether getting the wild type solid is the better use of the weeks left.

    On collecting: we have only got as far as tying the inflorescences to stakes (Figure 6). Bagging, when to cut, drying, and keeping lines from mixing are all things we have never done. We would rather learn that part standing next to you than look it up.

    會改變什麼：What it changes: 9 月起我們自己能不能不斷種源，以及鹽逆境曲線上要不要加突變株當參考。 Whether we can keep our own seed supply going from September, and whether a mutant line joins the salinity curve as a reference.
