The map

Almost every farm in Taiwan is a small farm.

We mapped every farm parcel under two hectares, then mapped how hard the weather swings. On this map, 97% of the farmland is under two hectares. On the ground that swings hardest it is 99.8%. There is no other kind of farmer to build for.
